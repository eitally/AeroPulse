#### Overall Impression

THRESH has real physiological depth and an authentic `#1 Stadium Track` visual signature on the surface, but additive feature growth left inner views overloaded (`6/8` cognitive-load failures), split across two eras of color tokens, and vulnerable at its highest-stakes mobile moment (trackside timing inside a dismissible modal). Consolidating the daily `Today` flow, elevating Trackside Mode into a resilient full-screen tool, and finishing the Pine/Chalk/Volt token migration will make the entire product feel as sharp as its emblem.

#### What's Working

1. **Parametric `#1 Stadium Track` Lockup & WCAG-Clamped Team Co-Branding (`AeroPulseLogo.tsx:169-272`, `TeamManagementPanel.tsx:888-991`)**: The 4-lane U-bend threshold gauge + `-14deg` wordmark and automatic contrast clamping for school colors deliver emotional team identity while keeping `Z1–Z6` physiological zone colors protected.
2. **Two-Phase Read-Only Strava Consent & Seed-Mark Calibration (`StravaAuthGate.tsx:893-942, 1153-1169, 1335-1405`)**: Decoupling identity from sync window selection, catching missing `activity:read` scopes with a 1-click re-auth recovery, and translating plain race marks (`5K 18:30`, `FTP 265W`) into live VDOT/zones earn immediate athlete trust.
3. **3-Channel Orthopedic Guardrails & Constructive Injury Swaps (`FitnessHealthView.tsx:1000-1365`, `CircadianFirstTouchHero.tsx:446-550`)**: Separating impact running strain from central/cycling load—and pairing `BONE_STRESS_STOP` alerts with "Virtual Miles" cross-training equivalents—turns sports science into humane daily coaching.

#### Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Trackside Watchless Split Pad lives inside an unmounting modal with ~28–30px tap targets and zero state persistence**
   - **Why it matters**: In `AthleteRosterModal.tsx:174-179, 337, 1296-1391` and `App.tsx:5336`, stopwatch state lives in ephemeral modal `useState`. Reaching it on mobile takes 4 steps, `+ Tap Split` buttons are `~28–30px` tall, and one stray thumb tap on the modal backdrop unmounts the component and wipes an entire workout's live splits.
   - **Fix**: Hoist stopwatch state to persistent storage (`localStorage` + live Volt timer pill in `App.tsx` header), promote Trackside Split Pad to a 1-tap full-screen mobile view with `>=56px` runner tap rows and undo-last-split, and require confirmation before `Reset` or discard.
   - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

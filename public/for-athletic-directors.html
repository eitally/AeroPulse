3. Student Privacy, SafeSport & District IT Checklists

  • No Mandatory Strava or Social Feeds: Student-athletes sign in via school Google or email; Strava is completely optional and never required for team participation.
  • COPPA <13 & Parent/Guardian Consent: Under-13 self-registration is hard-blocked; ages 13–17 trigger verifiable guardian notice and non-destructive revoke/delete controls.
  • SafeSport Body Weight Shield: Body weight and BMI metrics are locked hidden across all high school and minor views.
  • MAAPP Anti-1-on-1 Communication: Unmonitored private coach-to-minor direct messaging is prevented; workout feedback includes transparent guardian CC and immutable governance audit logging (ORG_ADMIN & ATHLETIC_TRAINER roles supported).
  • FERPA / SOPIPA / CA AB-1584: District-owned student records with zero advertising, zero behavioral profiling, and zero third-party data sales.

MeetReady Coach User Guide

Everything a high school, collegiate, or club coach needs to set up a roster in two minutes, protect student-athlete health every morning, prescribe workouts at any venue, time track splits without GPS watches, and sync directly with Athletic.net or TFRRS.

1. How to Onboard Your Team

Open Coach Mode from the top bar (click Coach next to the logo) and switch to the Roster tab. Creating your workspace takes three inputs.

Step 1 — Pick Type & Name

Click Create Team, select High School XC / Track, Collegiate, or Running Club, and enter your School or Club Name. High School programs automatically enable the U18 Safe-Sport Weight Shield and Parent CC. Colors and crest uploads stay tucked behind an optional toggle.

Step 2 — Share QR or Code

MeetReady immediately creates your 6-character Team Code (e.g. WGHS-26) and printable Locker-Room QR Poster. Athletes scan the poster or enter the code once during Google, Email, or Strava sign-in to claim their spot.

Step 3 — Invite or Assign Groups

Paste names/emails or upload a .CSV in Invite or Import Athletes (optional SeedMark auto-places everyone into VDOT pace pods before day one). Set each runner's Event Group (Sprints, 800m / Mile, 3000m / 3200m+) in the Roster table.

2. How to Assess Athlete Performance & Safety

Instead of chasing spreadsheet logs or text messages before practice, coaches get an automatic daily exception scan on every connected athlete.

  1. 2-Tap Athlete Check-In & Automatic Recovery Swaps: After training, athletes tap their RPE (1–10) and flag any sore area (e.g. Shin, Achilles, Knee). If they mark a flag, MeetReady automatically swaps their next run into a Low-Impact Recovery Flush and alerts the coach.
  2. Morning Squad Triage Banner: Whenever any athlete logs pain or high strain, a yellow alert bar appears right at the top of Coach Clipboard. Click it to jump straight into Squad Triage (7d).
  3. Inspect Safely & Return in 1 Click: In Squad Triage, compare 7-day compliance dots, last RPE, and SafeSport traffic lights. Click Inspect to open an athlete's personal readiness view, then click Back to Coach Roster at the top bar when done. High school rosters mask body weight and BMI automatically; collegiate rosters show Weekly Practice Hours (20h Cap).

3. How to Create Workouts & Season Plans

Write a single day's multi-set practice in seconds on the Clipboard, or publish multi-week training blocks to specific event cohorts.

Clipboard Quick Shorthand

Select Track / Run, Cycling, or Gym / Lift, tap an audience pill (e.g. 800m / Mile), and type natural shorthand like the example below. Tap Parse to build structured sets and auto-scaled Pace Pods immediately.

Venues & Doubles (AM / PM)

Tap the summary bar Schedule, Time & Venue when you need to change practice time, set an AM / PM doubles slot, or pick a school location (e.g. School Track, Foothills XC Park, Weight Room).

2x200m R + 4x800m CV + 2x200m R

To lay out a full season or 2-week block, click Build Cohort Plan in the Coach header, choose your archetype (800m, 1500m / Mile, 3200m, 5000m, or Cycling), pick a Rolling 1W, Rolling 2W, or Full Block publish window plus Target Cohort, and click publish. Use Back to Coach Workspace in the top bar anytime to return to Team Plan.

4. Review Performance, Fitness & Strain After Workouts

Athletes don't need GPS watches on track days or race nights. Capture live trackside splits in MeetReady, or review synced Strava/wearable fitness and strain after every session.

  • Watchless 0.01s Trackside Stopwatch: From Clipboard (Launch in Stopwatch) or the top Stopwatch tab, tap runner cards as athletes cross the line. MeetReady compares each lap to that runner's VDOT pod target and warns on heat/humidity or sudden late-rep slowdowns. Tap Save Workout Splits to MeetReady when finished to post splits and training load straight onto each athlete's log.
  • Inspect Post-Workout Fitness, Fatigue & Streams: In Coach Roster -> click Inspect on any athlete to inspect their Today Readiness, 1Hz activity breakdown, Running/Cycling Decoupling (Pa:Hr / Pw:Hr), 42d Fitness (CTL), 7d Fatigue (ATL), and Form (TSB). For school record boards and DMR / 4x400m lineups, expand Relay Optimizer & NFHS FAT Top Times inside Clipboard. Use Workout Library to reuse saved team workouts and post Guardian-CC'd comments in Squad Triage.

5. Integrate With Athletic.net, TFRRS & Parent Hub

Click Athletic.net / TFRRS in the Coach Workspace header to keep your MeetReady roster, meet calendar, and official marks aligned with public systems of record—even when sites block automated server bots.

Team URL & .ics Calendar

Paste your Athletic.net /team/<id>/xc or TFRRS team link and connect a live .ics / webcal:// meet feed (or drop a downloaded .ics file). Classic Bay Area & NCAA distances like Crystal Springs 2.95 mi (4,748m) and NCAA 8K convert automatically.

Select-All Paste, CSV & Hy-Tek

Copy any roster table, TFRRS CSV (FR-1..SR-4), Hy-Tek ; export, or multi-school meet results page and paste it straight in. Enter your school name in Team Filter so only your runners import, matching "Last, First" names to VDOT zones automatically.

1-Click Clipper & Parent Hub

Drag the Send to MeetReady bookmarklet to your bookmarks bar to capture Athletic.net or TFRRS tables from an open browser tab in one tap. Enable the Clean-Room Parent Hub link (/team/<slug>) to share meet dates and official FAT marks with families—never emails, GPS routes, HRV, RPE, or weight.

THRESH — Precision Endurance Physiology & Team Operations

THRESH is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA) unifying laboratory-grade running and cycling physiology, closed-loop daily workout adaptivity, and high school or collegiate team operations in one workspace. Strava OAuth serves as your read-only cross-device identity, followed by an explicit post-auth choice of what syncs to THRESH, with Garmin Connect and Intervals.icu available anytime as optional overnight HRV and Sleep add-ons.

2. Strava-First Onboarding, Sync Windows & Team Join Flow

Every individual athlete, coach, and student-athlete enters THRESH through a transparent 3-step onboarding flow—with zero passwords, zero unconsented background imports, and zero mandatory secondary integrations:

Step 1 · Continue With Strava (Primary Identity)

  • Universal Identity: Deterministic HMAC-SHA256 session derivation (deriveStravaSessionSid) restores your encrypted vault and team memberships across laptop and mobile browsers.
  • Inline Team Join: Athletes arriving via /join/<slug>, QR poster scan, or a 6-character Team Code see a live preview of their school/club crest before signing in with Strava.
  • Read-Only Pledge: THRESH never posts to your Strava feed, never modifies activity titles, and lets you revoke access in 1 click.

Step 2 · Choose What Syncs From Strava

  • Last 90 Days (Recommended): Instant 42-day CTL, 7-day ATL, and Form (TSB) calibration.
  • Last 6 Months / Full Archive: Deep seasonal CP/W' and durability curves.
  • Identity Only (No Workouts): Uses Strava strictly for sign-in; syncs zero workouts.
  • Sport, Units & Optional Seed Mark: Pre-fills mi / lbs or km / kg from Strava and optionally calibrates LT Pace / VDOT or Cycling FTP from a recent race mark (e.g., 18:45 5K, 10:15 3200m, 280W 20m).

Step 3 · Optional Overnight Sleep & HRV Add-Ons (Garmin Connect & Intervals.icu)

Connecting Garmin Connect or Intervals.icu unlocks overnight HRV rMSSD, Sleep Score, and Resting HR autonomic readiness morphing—but is 100% optional. Athletes training with Strava alone get full Banister CTL/ATL/TSB, two-pass calibrated running rTSS and Grade-Adjusted Efficiency Factor (EF), GPS segment PRs, VDOT pods, and structured workout planning on Day 1.

3. Circadian Readiness, 3-Channel Orthopedic Strip & Plan vs. Actual Ledger

The Today workspace adapts to your circadian training cycle—presenting your morning readiness brief and daily prescription before training, and transitioning into a Session Receipt & Compliance Summary once your workout is logged.

3-Channel Orthopedic Load Strip

Decomposes 7-day training strain into three independent biological vectors:

  • Central Autonomic Load: Cardiorespiratory and HRV stress across all sports.
  • Eccentric Run Impact: Tibial/Achilles ground-reaction force (Run and Track volume weighted by intensity).
  • Concentric Bike Torque: Muscular force without osteogenic impact.

When Eccentric Run Impact ACWR exceeds 1.28x while Central ACWR remains healthy, THRESH offers a 1-tap Zero-Impact Z2 Ride / Pool Swap.

Plan vs. Actual Weekly Ledger

Whenever morning HRV drops below your 60-day corridor or TSB plunges below -28, THRESH computes a 7-day rolling schedule adjustment:

- Thu: 5x1km VO2max Track (Running · 55m · 82 TSS) + Thu: Modulated Threshold Cruise (Running · 45m · 64 TSS) Net Delta: ΔTSS -18 | ΔCTL -0.4 | ΔTSB +2.1

Click Commit Adjusted Week to Plan to update your calendar in one transaction.

4. 1Hz Stream Inspector, Post-Workout Debrief & Unstructured Rep Recovery

Drop .FIT, .FIT.GZ, .GPX, .TCX, or bulk .ZIP archives directly into THRESH. A dedicated Web Worker (activityProcessor.worker.ts) upsamples and analyzes 1Hz streams off the main thread.

  • Interactive Drag-to-Zoom (UPlotStreamChart): Drag across any time window on the 1Hz canvas to compute instantaneous live slice metrics (Avg W, NP, W/kg, Avg/Max HR, Pace, GAP, Cadence, ΔElev, and W' Depletion).
  • 30-Second Post-Workout Debrief: Automatically extracts 3–4 timestamped coaching insights (e.g., "Cardiac Drift Alert (+6.2% Pa:Hr after 42:00)", "Deep W' Match Burn (18% remaining)", "Fastest 60s HR Recovery (-34 bpm)") with 1-click chart zoom.
  • 5-Second Post-Workout Quick-Log: Stamp Borg RPE (1–10), Feel (STRONG/NORMAL/HEAVY/DEPLETED), g/hr Carbs, mg/hr Sodium, and Gear onto any workout.
  • 1Hz Unstructured Interval Auto-Detector ("Forgot-to-Lap Recovery"): Forgot to press the watch Lap button during 8 x 400m or a Fartlek? Click Recover Unstructured Reps in the Activity Inspector. THRESH runs bimodal quantile (P30/P82/P92) hysteresis segmentation on 5-second smoothed velocity/power streams, recovers every work rep down to 18s (200m track reps), and snaps standard track distances (~200m, ~400m, ~800m, ~1000m, ~1600m).

5. Empirical 1Hz CP/W' & Fatigue-Sliced kJ/kg / kcal Durability

Two athletes can share the exact same fresh 20-minute FTP (300W) or 5K PR (15:30), yet diverge dramatically after 2 hours of racing. In Progress → Power & Pace Curves, THRESH slices your historical 1Hz streams by accumulated mechanical work performed prior to each effort:

Cycling Work Buckets (0–40 kJ/kg)

Evaluates Peak 5m and 20m power at 0 (Fresh), 10, 20, 30, and 40 kJ/kg of prior mechanical work.

Running Metabolic Buckets (0–2,000 kcal)

Evaluates Peak 5m and 20m Grade-Adjusted Pace (GAP) at 0 (Fresh), 500, 1,000, 1,500, and 2,000 kcal of prior expenditure.

Classifies your phenotype into PRO_TOUR_RESILIENT (≥ 95% retention at deep fatigue), WELL_CONDITIONED, MODERATE_FADE, or GLYCOGEN_FRAGILE, and prescribes exact intra-session carbohydrate oxidation targets (60–110 g/hr).

6. Course Physics, Weather, Altitude & Carb/Sodium Race Simulator

Located in Progress → Goals & Race Sim, the Race Simulator models your target event across 5 course segments by combining:

  • Gravitational & Aerodynamic Grade Cost: Minetti metabolic cost of uphill/downhill running and cycling climbing resistance.
  • Thermal Derating (> 18°C): Quantifies skin blood-flow shunting and cardiac drift penalty per degree Celsius above optimal (12–16°C).
  • Hypoxic Altitude Derating (> 1,000m): Adjusts sustainable aerobic fraction for elevation.
  • Fueling & Hydration Prescription: Outputs total and hourly Dual-Source Carbohydrates (1:0.8 glucose:fructose for > 90 g/hr, 2:1 for 60–90 g/hr), Sodium (mg/hr), Fluid (mL/hr), Gel equivalents, and pre-race Caffeine (3 mg/kg).

7. GPS-to-Official Chip/FAT Rubber-Banding & THV-... Passports

GPS watches routinely over-read track and road courses by 1.2%–2.5% (e.g., logging 5,085m for a certified 5,000m), distorting lap paces. Inside Log Official Race Result:

  • Import results from Athletic.net, TFRRS, MileSplit, or RunSignup, or log a watchless 0.01s FAT track race.
  • Enter your watch’s raw GPS distance alongside the certified USATF/World Athletics distance: THRESH rubber-bands every split to the exact certified course markers and generates a shareable THV-... Verified Race Passport with a deterministic FNV-1a verification hash.

8. Auto-Scaled VDOT Pacing Pods & Persistent Watchless Split Pad

High school and collegiate cross-country and track coaches manage 25 to 80+ athletes spanning a wide range of VDOT scores. Open the Trackside Split Pad directly from the header or Athletes & Coach Deck:

1. Auto-Clustered VDOT Pacing Pods

Select one workout intensity (Easy, Marathon, Threshold / Tinman CV, VO2max Interval, or Mile Repetition), rep count, and rep distance (200m–1600m). THRESH clusters your squad into Pods A–D (≤ 3.0 VDOT spread / ±2s per 400m) with exact 200m, 400m, 800m, 1000m, and 1600m splits, 1-Click Push to Pod Calendars, and Copy Printable Clipboard Sheet.

2. Persistent Trackside "Spikes-On" Split Pad

Athletes often race or run fast track intervals in spikes without GPS watches. Tap any runner row to start the persistent wall-clock 0.01s stopwatch—surviving modal closes, screen locks, and accidental page reloads—with Undo Last Split, confirmation-guarded Reset, and 1-click CSV export.

3. Team Branding Studio, Printable QR Posters, CSV Auto-Match & Sharing Tiers

  • WCAG-Clamped Team Co-Branding: Coaches launch a branded workspace with contrast-clamped school colors across light and dark modes, crest upload, and printable locker-room QR posters (/join/<slug>).
  • CSV Roster Auto-Match: Upload or paste Name, Email, Phone, Squad, GradYear, ParentEmail, SeedMark. When an invited athlete signs in with Strava, THRESH automatically matches their roster slot, squad pod, and seed mark.
  • Athlete Sharing Tiers: Athletes joining a team choose SUMMARY_ONLY (Recommended — shares CTL/ATL/TSB, 7d load & CARA hours), FULL_STREAMS (shares full workouts & calendar edits with coaches), or LOAD_ONLY (shares aggregate training load only).

9. Official NCAA Indoor/Outdoor/Altitude Track Conversions & Recruiting Standards

Inside Log Official Race Result → Quick Watchless FAT Logger, THRESH automatically indexes marks across:

  • NCAA Indoor Facility Multipliers: Converts between 200m Flat Indoor, 200m Banked/Oversized Indoor, and 400m Outdoor using gender- and event-specific NCAA indexing factors (e.g., Men's Flat-to-Banked: 800m ×0.9857, Mile/1500m ×0.9869, 3000m ×0.9885, 5000m ×0.9907; Women's: 800m ×0.9872, Mile/1500m ×0.9882, 3000m ×0.9896, 5000m ×0.9919).
  • NCAA Altitude Allowance (≥ 3,000 ft): Applies nonlinear aerobic time credit for marks run at elevation (e.g., Boulder 5,280 ft, Albuquerque 4,950 ft, Flagstaff 6,910 ft).
  • 6-Tier Recruiting & Championship Standards Matrix: Live progress bars and exact seconds-to-qualify gaps for Men's and Women's HS State Qualifier, NCAA D3 Impact Recruit, NCAA D2 Scholarship, HS Nationals, NCAA D1 Walk-On, and NCAA D1 Scholarship across 800m, 1500m, 1600m, Mile, 3000m, 3200m, 5000m, and 10000m.

10. Safe-Sport Weight Shield, Bone-Stress / Ferritin Guardrails & 20h CARA Audit

  • Safe-Sport / RED-S Protection (Hide Weight & W/kg): Enabled by default on U18 high school rosters and toggleable in Athlete Profile to mask body weight (kg/lbs) and W/kg across student-athlete UI views while keeping absolute power, pace, and heart rate zones calibrated underneath.
  • Focal Shin / Metatarsal Bone-Stress Injury (BSI) Stop: In the Student-Athlete Wellness Check-In on Today, reporting focal shin or foot bone tenderness (≥ 2/3) immediately triggers a red BONE_STRESS_STOP (0% impact intensity cap) and routes the athlete to zero-impact cross-training.
  • Serum Ferritin (ng/mL) & Academic Exam Modulation: Flags iron depletion when serum ferritin drops below 30 ng/mL (endurance hematology floor) and down-scales interval volume by 15–20% during Midterm/Finals weeks (Academic Stress 4–5 or Sleep < 6.2h).
  • Training Room Cross-Training → Virtual Running Miles: Converts Aqua Jogging (100% impact saved), AlterG (75% BW) (62% impact saved), Elliptical, Arc Trainer, and Stationary Bike minutes + RPE into equivalent virtual running miles/km and systemic TSS.
  • NCAA Bylaw 17.1 (20-Hour Weekly / 4-Hour Daily CARA) Audit: Automatically audits rolling 7-day countable hours across connected roster athletes in the Coach Deck.

1. Banister EWMA Load Model: CTL_t = CTL_{t-1} + (TSS_t - CTL_{t-1}) / 42 ATL_t = ATL_{t-1} + (TSS_t - ATL_{t-1}) / 7 TSB_t = CTL_t - ATL_t 2. Coggan Normalized Power & TSS: NP = [ (1/N) * Σ (Rolling_30s_Watts_i)^4 ]^(1/4) IF = NP / FTP TSS = (Duration_secs * NP * IF) / (FTP * 3600) * 100 3. Monod-Scherrer Critical Power (CP) & Skiba W' Balance: P(t) = CP + W' / t W'_bal(t) = W' - ∫ max(0, P(u) - CP) * exp(-(t - u) / τ_W') du 4. Minetti Grade-Adjusted Pace (GAP) Energy Cost: Cr(i) = 155.4*i^5 - 30.4*i^4 - 43.3*i^3 + 46.3*i^2 + 19.5*i + 3.6 (J/kg/m) 5. 12. REST API, Multi-Wearable Webhooks & AES-256-GCM Token Vault

THRESH enforces strict session isolation (HttpOnly signed thresh_session cookies + deterministic Strava athlete session derivation), an encrypted server-side OAuth Token Vault (AES-256-GCM in server/tokenVault.ts using TOKEN_VAULT_KEY / OAUTH_ENCRYPTION_KEY + serverless thresh_vault backup cookie), HMAC-signed OAuth CSRF state tokens, and a Clean-Room Provider Firewall (server/cleanRoomFirewall.ts) that strips third-party Strava-sourced records before any AI prompt or multi-user coach export.

Endpoint Method Description
/api/strava/auth/url & /callback GET Mints HMAC-signed Strava OAuth URL (thresh_oauth_state cookie), verifies callback state, binds deterministic Strava session ID, and preserves /join/:slug team claims.
/api/strava/sync-policy & /sync POST / GET Saves athlete sync window (90d, 180d, all, none) and syncs Strava workouts with after pagination, two-pass rTSS, and unchecked-scope safety.
/api/teams & /api/teams/join-claim POST / GET Creates school/club workspaces, previews /join/:slug invites, claims CSV roster slots on Strava sign-in, and enforces SUMMARY_ONLY / FULL_STREAMS / LOAD_ONLY coach projections.
/api/vault/status GET Returns current session’s encrypted provider connection metadata without exposing raw tokens.
/api/physiology/mmp-debrief POST Computes 1Hz Empirical MMP/GAP curves (CP/W', CS/D'), 3-Channel Orthopedic Load, and 30s Post-Workout Debrief.
/api/physiology/durability POST Computes 0–40 kJ/kg (Cycling) or 0–2,000 kcal (Running) fatigue-sliced durability curve.
/api/physiology/race-simulator POST Runs the 5-segment Course Physics, Weather/Altitude & Dual-Source Carb/Sodium Race Simulator.
/api/physiology/race-passport POST Rubber-bands raw GPS splits to certified USATF/World Athletics distance and mints a THV-... Verified Passport.
/api/webhooks/:provider POST First-party webhook ingestion for garmin, wahoo, coros, polar, oura, whoop, and apple-healthkit.
/api/workouts/export POST Compiles structured Intervals-syntax workouts into Apple WorkoutKit JSON, Garmin Training JSON, Zwift .ZWO, or .ERG.

CHAPTER 02 · CIRCADIAN TODAY LOOP

Circadian Readiness, 3-Channel Orthopedic Strip & Weekly Git-Diff (`10x #1` & `10x #3`)

The Today / Fitness workspace adapts to your circadian training cycle—presenting your morning readiness brief and workout morphing options before training, and transitioning into a Session Complete Compliance Banner once your workout is logged.

10x #1 · 3-Channel Orthopedic Load Strip

Decomposes 7-day training strain into three independent biological vectors:

  • Central Autonomic Load: Cardiorespiratory & HRV stress across all sports.
  • Eccentric Run Impact: Tibial/Achilles ground-reaction force (`Run` & `Track` volume weighted by intensity).
  • Concentric Bike Torque: Muscular force without osteogenic impact.

When Eccentric Run Impact ACWR exceeds 1.28x while Central ACWR remains healthy, THRESH offers a 1-tap Zero-Impact Z2 Ride / Pool Swap.

10x #3 · Weekly Training Git-Diff

Whenever morning HRV drops below your 60-day corridor or TSB plunges below -28, THRESH computes a 7-day rolling schedule diff:

- Thu: 5x1km VO2max Track (Running · 55m · 82 TSS) + Thu: Modulated Threshold Cruise (Running · 45m · 64 TSS) Net Delta: ΔTSS -18 | ΔCTL -0.4 | ΔTSB +2.1

Click Commit Weekly Git-Diff to Plan to update your calendar in one transaction.

CHAPTER 03 · 1HZ TELEMETRY & INTERVALS

1Hz Stream Inspector, 30s Post-Workout Debrief & Unstructured Rep Recovery

Drop `.FIT`, `.FIT.GZ`, `.GPX`, `.TCX`, or bulk `.ZIP` archives directly into THRESH. A dedicated Web Worker (`activityProcessor.worker.ts`) upsamples and analyzes 1Hz streams off the main thread.

  • Interactive Drag-to-Zoom (`UPlotStreamChart`): Drag across any time window on the 1Hz canvas to compute instantaneous live slice metrics (`Avg W`, `NP`, `W/kg`, `Avg/Max HR`, `Pace`, `GAP`, `Cadence`, `ΔElev`, and `W' Depletion`).
  • 30-Second Post-Workout Debrief: Automatically extracts 3–4 timestamped coaching insights (e.g., "Cardiac Drift Alert (+6.2% Pa:Hr after 42:00)", "Deep W' Match Burn (18% remaining)", "Fastest 60s HR Recovery (-34 bpm)") with 1-click chart zoom.
  • 5-Second Post-Workout Quick-Log: Stamp Borg RPE (`1–10`), Feel (`STRONG`/`NORMAL`/`HEAVY`/`DEPLETED`), `g/hr` Carbs, `mg/hr` Sodium, and Gear onto any workout.
  • 1Hz Unstructured Interval Auto-Detector ("Forgot-to-Lap Recovery"): Forgot to press the watch Lap button during 8 x 400m or a Fartlek? Click Recover Unstructured Reps (1Hz No-Lap Detector) in the Activity Modal. THRESH runs bimodal quantile (`P30`/`P82`/`P92`) hysteresis segmentation on 5-second smoothed velocity/power streams, recovers every work rep down to 18s (`200m` track reps), and snaps standard track distances (`~200m`, `~400m`, `~800m`, `~1000m`, `~1600m`).
CHAPTER 04 · PHYSIOLOGY LAB & DURABILITY

Empirical 1Hz `CP/W'` & Fatigue-Sliced `kJ/kg` / `kcal` Durability (`10x #2`)

Two athletes can share the exact same fresh 20-minute FTP (`300W`) or 5K PR (`15:30`), yet diverge dramatically after 2 hours of racing. In the Physiology / Curves view, THRESH slices your historical 1Hz streams by accumulated mechanical work performed prior to each effort:

Cycling Work Buckets (`0–40 kJ/kg`)

Evaluates Peak 5m and 20m power at 0 (Fresh), 10, 20, 30, and 40 kJ/kg of prior mechanical work.

Running Metabolic Buckets (`0–2,000 kcal`)

Evaluates Peak 5m and 20m Grade-Adjusted Pace (`GAP`) at 0 (Fresh), 500, 1,000, 1,500, and 2,000 kcal of prior expenditure.

Classifies your phenotype into PRO_TOUR_RESILIENT (`≥ 95%` retention at deep fatigue), WELL_CONDITIONED, MODERATE_FADE, or GLYCOGEN_FRAGILE, and prescribes exact intra-session carbohydrate oxidation targets (`60–110 g/hr`).

CHAPTER 05 · COURSE & FUELING SIMULATOR

Course Physics, Weather, Altitude & Carb/Sodium Race Simulator (`10x #4`)

Located in Goals & Totals, the Race Simulator models your target event across 5 course segments by combining:

  • Gravitational & Aerodynamic Grade Cost: Minetti metabolic cost of uphill/downhill running and cycling climbing resistance.
  • Thermal Derating (`> 18°C`): Quantifies skin blood-flow shunting and cardiac drift penalty per degree Celsius above optimal (`12–16°C`).
  • Hypoxic Altitude Derating (`> 1,000m`): Adjusts sustainable aerobic fraction for elevation.
  • Fueling & Hydration Prescription: Outputs total and hourly Dual-Source Carbohydrates (`1:0.8` glucose:fructose for `> 90 g/hr`, `2:1` for `60–90 g/hr`), Sodium (`mg/hr`), Fluid (`mL/hr`), Gel equivalents, and pre-race Caffeine (`3 mg/kg`).
CHAPTER 06 · OFFICIAL FAT & VERIFIED PASSPORTS

GPS-to-Official Chip/FAT Rubber-Banding & `THV-...` Passports (`10x #6`)

GPS watches routinely over-read track and road courses by `1.2%–2.5%` (e.g., logging `5,085m` for a certified `5,000m`), distorting lap paces. Inside Log Official Race Result:

  • Import results from Athletic.net, TFRRS, MileSplit, or RunSignup, or log a watchless 0.01s FAT track race.
  • Enter your watch’s raw GPS distance alongside the certified USATF/World Athletics distance: THRESH rubber-bands every split to the exact certified course markers and generates a shareable `THV-...` Verified Race Passport with a deterministic FNV-1a verification hash.
CHAPTER 07 · HIGH SCHOOL & COLLEGIATE COACH DECK

Auto-Scaled VDOT Pacing Pods & Watchless Multi-Athlete Split Pad

High school and collegiate cross-country/track coaches manage 25 to 80+ athletes spanning a wide range of VDOT scores. Open Athletes, Friends & Coaching Hub → HS & College Coach Deck:

1. Auto-Clustered VDOT Pacing Pods

Select one workout intensity (Easy, Marathon, Threshold / Tinman CV, VO2max Interval, or Mile Repetition), rep count, and rep distance (200m–1600m). THRESH clusters your squad into Pods A–D (`≤ 3.0 VDOT` spread / `±2s` per 400m) with exact `200m`, `400m`, `800m`, `1000m`, and `1600m` splits, 1-Click Push to Pod Calendars, and Copy Printable Clipboard Sheet.

2. Trackside "Spikes-On / Watchless" Split Pad

Athletes often race or run fast track intervals in spikes without GPS watches. Start the built-in 0.01s gun stopwatch and tap + Tap Split on any athlete row as they cross the line to capture cumulative and per-lap splits, then export all splits as CSV in one click.

CHAPTER 08 · NCAA TRACK INDEXING & RECRUITING

Official NCAA Indoor/Outdoor/Altitude Track Conversions & Recruiting Standards

Inside Log Official Race Result → Quick Watchless FAT Logger, THRESH automatically indexes marks across:

  • NCAA Indoor Facility Multipliers: Converts between 200m Flat Indoor, 200m Banked/Oversized Indoor, and 400m Outdoor using gender- and event-specific NCAA indexing factors (e.g., Men's Flat-to-Banked: `800m ×0.9857`, `Mile/1500m ×0.9869`, `3000m ×0.9885`, `5000m ×0.9907`; Women's: `800m ×0.9872`, `Mile/1500m ×0.9882`, `3000m ×0.9896`, `5000m ×0.9919`).
  • NCAA Altitude Allowance (`≥ 3,000 ft`): Applies nonlinear aerobic time credit for marks run at elevation (e.g., Boulder `5,280 ft`, Albuquerque `4,950 ft`, Flagstaff `6,910 ft`).
  • 6-Tier Recruiting & Championship Standards Matrix: Live progress bars and exact seconds-to-qualify gaps for Men's and Women's HS State Qualifier, NCAA D3 Impact Recruit, NCAA D2 Scholarship, HS Nationals (Nike / New Balance), NCAA D1 Walk-On, and NCAA D1 Scholarship across `800m`, `1500m`, `1600m`, `Mile`, `3000m`, `3200m`, `5000m`, and `10000m`.
CHAPTER 09 · SAFE-SPORT, BSI & NCAA CARA

Safe-Sport Weight Shield, Bone-Stress / Ferritin Guardrails & 20h CARA Audit

  • Safe-Sport / RED-S Protection (`Hide Weight & W/kg`): Toggle in Athlete Profile to mask body weight (`kg`/`lbs`) and `W/kg` across student-athlete UI views while keeping absolute power, pace, and heart rate zones calibrated underneath.
  • Focal Shin / Metatarsal Bone-Stress Injury (BSI) Stop: In the 5-Second Student-Athlete Check-In on Today/Fitness, reporting focal shin or foot bone tenderness (`≥ 2/3`) immediately triggers a red BONE_STRESS_STOP (`0%` impact intensity cap) and routes the athlete to zero-impact cross-training.
  • Serum Ferritin (`ng/mL`) & Academic Exam Modulation: Flags iron depletion when serum ferritin drops below `30 ng/mL` (endurance hematology floor) and down-scales interval volume by `15–20%` during Midterm/Finals weeks (`Academic Stress 4–5` or `Sleep < 6.2h`).
  • Training Room Cross-Training → Virtual Running Miles: Converts Aqua Jogging (`100%` impact saved), AlterG (75% BW) (`62%` impact saved), Elliptical, Arc Trainer, and Stationary Bike minutes + RPE into equivalent virtual running miles/km and systemic TSS.
  • NCAA Bylaw 17.1 (`20-Hour Weekly / 4-Hour Daily CARA`) Audit: Automatically audits rolling 7-day countable hours across connected roster athletes in the Coach Deck.

CHAPTER 11 · REST API & SECURITY ARCHITECTURE

Server Endpoints, Multi-Wearable Webhooks, OTA Export & AES-256-GCM Token Vault

THRESH enforces strict session isolation (`HttpOnly` signed cookies), an encrypted server-side OAuth Token Vault (`AES-256-GCM` in `server/tokenVault.ts`), and a Clean-Room Provider Firewall (`server/cleanRoomFirewall.ts`) that strips third-party `Strava`-sourced records before any AI prompt or multi-user coach export.

Endpoint Method Description
/api/vault/status GET Returns current session’s encrypted provider connection metadata without exposing raw tokens.
/api/physiology/mmp-debrief POST Computes 1Hz Empirical MMP/GAP curves (`CP/W'`, `CS/D'`), 3-Channel Orthopedic Load, and 30s Post-Workout Debrief.
/api/physiology/durability POST Computes `0–40 kJ/kg` (Cycling) or `0–2,000 kcal` (Running) fatigue-sliced durability curve (`10x #2`).
/api/physiology/race-simulator POST Runs the 5-segment Course Physics, Weather/Altitude & Dual-Source Carb/Sodium Race Simulator (`10x #4`).
/api/physiology/race-passport POST Rubber-bands raw GPS splits to certified USATF/World Athletics distance and mints a `THV-...` Verified Passport (`10x #6`).
/api/webhooks/:provider POST First-party webhook ingestion for `garmin`, `wahoo`, `coros`, `polar`, `oura`, `whoop`, and `apple-healthkit`.
/api/workouts/export POST Compiles structured Intervals-syntax workouts into Apple `WorkoutKit` JSON, Garmin Training JSON, Zwift `.ZWO`, or `.ERG`.

Governing use of MeetReady readiness modeling, trackside timing, workout planning, and scholastic/collegiate coaching workspaces.

1. Informational & Non-Medical Disclaimer

Not Medical Advice: MeetReady metrics (CTL, ATL, TSB, ACWR, HRV trends, cardiac drift, iron/ferritin reserve reminders, and training modulation) are informational endurance coaching tools only. MeetReady is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or injury. Consult a licensed physician or certified athletic trainer before making medical or return-to-play decisions.

2. Eligibility, Youth Accounts & SafeSport Governance

  • Users must be at least 13 years of age (COPPA minimum). Athletes ages 13–17 require verified parent/guardian acknowledgment before accessing full team sync beyond Safe-Sport quarantine.
  • Coaches and staff agree to abide by applicable school district, NCAA Bylaw 17.1 CARA, and U.S. Center for SafeSport Minor Athlete Abuse Prevention Policies (MAAPP). Attempting to bypass guardian visibility on minor workout comments or contact minors privately outside guardian-CC'd team channels is prohibited.
  • Only authorized coaching staff (HEAD_COACH, ASSISTANT_COACH) may program or broadcast team workouts; ATHLETIC_TRAINER and ORG_ADMIN roles carry scoped safeguarding, audit, or governance permissions.

3. Athlete Data Ownership & Third-Party Connectors

  • Athlete Ownership: You retain ownership of all workout telemetry and manual logs you upload or record. You may export or delete your account data at any time.
  • Supported Connectors: MeetReady integrates with user-authorized accounts from Strava, Garmin Connect, Intervals.icu (including Intervals.icu-relayed devices such as COROS and WHOOP), Wahoo, Polar, and direct .FIT/.GPX/.TCX/.ZIP file imports. MeetReady is not affiliated with or endorsed by those third-party brands.

4. Institutional Subscriptions & PO Billing

Individual athletes and parents/guardians receive free core access. Institutional licenses for High Schools, Collegiate Programs, Clubs, and School Districts follow transparent published rates on /procurement with annual Purchase Order (PO), ASB check, or invoice billing.

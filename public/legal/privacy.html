MeetReady Privacy Policy & Youth Data Protection

Applies to individual athletes, coaches, parents/guardians, high school programs, collegiate programs, and clubs across MeetReady web and PWA surfaces.

1. Data We Collect & Strict Minimization

  • Account Identity: Display name, sign-in identifier (Google OAuth, WebAuthn Passkey credential, verified email for OTP/Magic Link, or optional Strava athlete ID), and explicit role (ATHLETE, HEAD_COACH, ASSISTANT_COACH, ORG_ADMIN, ATHLETIC_TRAINER, or PARENT_FOLLOWER).
  • Birth Month & Year Only (YYYY-MM): MeetReady intentionally avoids collecting full calendar birth dates for minor age-tier routing. Age verification computes age tiers strictly from month/year boundaries.
  • Endurance Telemetry & Manual Logs: Workout duration, distance, splits, power/pace, heart rate, trackside stopwatch splits, planned sessions, and voluntary post-session check-ins (RPE & soreness/pain flags).
  • Optional Body Mass & Safe-Sport Shield: When an athlete is under 18 (isMinorU18) or joins a HIGH_SCHOOL team, Safe-Sport weight suppression (safeSportHideWeight) is locked ON and body mass / W/kg metrics are excluded from team projections and reports.

2. Children Under 13 (COPPA) & Youth Athletes Ages 13–17

No Unmanaged Child Accounts: Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. § 6501 et seq.), MeetReady does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age and hard-blocks account creation for under-13 birthdates.

  • Verifiable Parent / Guardian Consent (Ages 13–17 & High School Programs): High school and U18 minor memberships remain in pending-consent quarantine (LOAD_ONLY load & readiness triage summary only, zero AI access, zero weight exposure) until a parent/guardian receives and confirms the single-use consent link.
  • Hashed Consent Tokens & Instant Revocation: Parent consent tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes at rest and never exposed back to student-athlete browsers. Parents can click Decline & Delete Child Data at any time to purge minor team telemetry immediately.
  • SafeSport MAAPP Anti-1-on-1 Communication: Un-CC'd direct 1-on-1 SMS links and private coach-to-minor workout comment threads are blocked on the server (403 Forbidden). Minor workout comments automatically apply visible parent/guardian CC and audit logging.
  • Automatic Age-18 Graduation Transfer: Upon reaching 18 years of age (FERPA rights transfer) or graduating on July 15, adult athletes control guardian visibility and retain portable personal workout & stopwatch history.

3. SOPIPA, California AB-1584 (Ed Code § 49073.1) & FERPA

  • Zero Advertising & Zero Data Sales: Student and athlete records are never sold, rented, or used for targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or third-party ad networks.
  • School Official & LEA Ownership: When provisioned by a school or district, pupil records remain under the legal control of the Educational Agency in compliance with FERPA and California Education Code § 49073.1 (AB-1584) / SOPIPA.
  • FERPA Directory Opt-Out: Coaches and school administrators control public meet leaderboard opt-outs for /team/:slug; public hubs never expose athlete emails, phone numbers, guardian emails, GPS routes, HRV, sleep, RPE, or weight.

4. Strava Clean-Room Firewall & Optional Gemini AI

MeetReady enforces a strict server-side telemetry allowlist firewall before any prompt reaches Google Gemini AI:

  • 100% Strava Exclusion: Activities originating from Strava (across all payload keys including recentWorkouts) are stripped unconditionally and never transmitted to Gemini AI or displayed inside unowned third-party views.
  • Minor AI Default-Off: AI Coach insights are disabled by default for all minors (U18) and high school athletes, and body weight / body composition fields are stripped server-side.
  • Strict Read-Only Coach Scope: Coaches only see physiology tiers explicitly authorized by the athlete's per-team sharing setting (LOAD_ONLY, SUMMARY_ONLY, or FULL_STREAMS) and privacy shield toggles (Share Wellness & HRV, Share GPS).

5. Security Controls, Encryption & Permanent Erasure

  • Encryption: Credentials and tenant vaults are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM; PostgreSQL connections enforce strict verified TLS in production; FIDO2/WebAuthn Passkeys verify cryptographic signatures before issuing sessions.
  • Self-Serve Deletion: Athletes can revoke Strava/wearable connectors or execute permanent erasure via Account & Privacy → Delete My Account, which wipes session cookies, team memberships, and encrypted vault state (preventing deletion only if a sole active Head Coach has not transferred ownership).

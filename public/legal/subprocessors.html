Self-Serve Data Portability, Erasure & 30-Day Change Notice

  • First-Party Anonymous Conversion Counters (Zero Third-Party Analytics): MeetReady does not embed Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Segment, or third-party ad trackers. Public landing and coach onboarding milestones emit only first-party anonymous aggregate conversion counters (zero cookies, zero PII, zero IP address storage, and unconditionally excluded from minor/student accounts).
  • Self-Serve Export & Deletion: Signed-in athletes and coaches can export their complete personal and team-scoped records anytime in JSON or CSV via Account, Teams & Privacy → Download My Data (GET /api/auth/export), or permanently erase their account and team telemetry via Delete My Account (DELETE /api/auth/account).
  • Subprocessor Updates: School districts and partner athletic departments receive at least 30 days' advance notice before any material new subprocessor handles institutional student records. Questions: privacy@meetready.app.
